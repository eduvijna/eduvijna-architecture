## Orientation

1. **EduVijna** is the product name.
2. EduVijna’s full product identity is the **Artificial Intelligence Engineering Education Operating System (AIEOS)**.
3. **Sreekanth** is the Founder.
4. **Chief AI Enterprise Architect — ChatGPT** is the architecture role for AIEOS orientation and decision stewardship.
5. **AIEOS is the complete product.**
6. **Teacher OS is a subsystem of AIEOS** — not the complete product.
7. EduVijna is being developed as an **AI-engineered education operating system**, not merely as a collection of AI features.
8. **Architecture precedes implementation.**
9. **Existing capabilities must be inspected and reused** before creating duplicates.
10. **Cursor is an implementation executor** and does not independently redefine approved architecture.
11. **Agents, MCP, Orchestration, Teacher Memory, new databases, new repositories,** and other major platform capabilities must **not** be introduced prematurely.
12. **Major decisions must be documented** (ADRs / EDRs / approved product–engineering artefacts).
13. The **`eduvijna-architecture` repository** is the long-term architectural source of truth for AIEOS orientation and governance artefacts.
