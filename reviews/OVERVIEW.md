# Reviews — Overview

## Purpose

Capture architecture and design review requests, evidence, outcomes, and follow-up actions for EduVijna initiatives.

## Scope

- Review intake records, checklists, and outcome notes when authorised
- Traceability between reviewed proposals and resulting decisions or remediation work
- EAO-facilitated review artefacts that support governance

## Ownership

EduVijna Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO), coordinating with requesting product and engineering teams.

## Contents

Bootstrap placeholder only (`OVERVIEW.md`). No review records or checklists are created in EBP-001 beyond repository contribution templates.

## Exclusions

- Code review comments belonging to application repositories
- Performance test raw data unless summarised as review evidence under EAO process
- ADRs (see `decisions/`) and standards (see `standards/`)
- Untracked verbal reviews with no written outcome
