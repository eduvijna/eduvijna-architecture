# Standards — Overview

## Purpose

Maintain the library of engineering and architecture standards that teams across the EduVijna ecosystem are expected to follow.

## Scope

- Authorised standards for engineering practice, interoperability, security, data, and related domains
- Standard lifecycle artefacts (proposal, approval, versioning)
- Cross-repository guidance owned by the EAO

## Ownership

EduVijna Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO), with subject-matter owners assigned per standard when published.

## Contents

Approved and in-progress standards under EAO stewardship.

## Exclusions

- Team-local conventions that are not elevated to enterprise standards
- Architecture designs and ADRs (see `architecture/` and `decisions/`)
- Product requirements and sprint backlogs
- Vendor documentation reproduced without EAO adoption
