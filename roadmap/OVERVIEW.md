# Roadmap — Overview

## Purpose

Hold the enterprise architecture and transformation roadmap for the EduVijna ecosystem, connecting discovery findings and decisions to sequenced change.

## Scope

- Authorised roadmap artefacts for capabilities, platforms, and architecture initiatives
- Sequencing, dependencies, and horizon planning owned by the EAO
- Links from roadmap items to blueprints, reviews, and delivery programmes

## Ownership

EduVijna Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO), aligned with product and engineering leadership priorities.

Bootstrap placeholder only (`OVERVIEW.md`). No roadmap documents are created in EBP-001.

## Exclusions

- Product feature backlogs and sprint boards
- Project plans owned solely by delivery teams without EAO roadmap adoption
- Architecture specifications and standards (see `architecture/` and `standards/`)
- Discovery raw notes not yet synthesised into roadmap inputs
