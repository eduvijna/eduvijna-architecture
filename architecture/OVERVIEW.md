# Architecture — Overview

## Purpose

Store architecture artefacts that describe how the EduVijna ecosystem is structured and intended to evolve.

## Scope

- Reference, current-state, and target-state architecture artefacts
- Views, models, and architecture narratives governed by EAO process
- Material that depends on discovery and decision outcomes where required

## Ownership

EduVijna Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO). Architecture artefacts require EAO review before they are treated as authoritative.

## Contents

Approved and in-progress architecture artefacts under EAO stewardship.

## Exclusions

- Application source code and service implementation details
- Standards definitions (see `standards/`)
- Decision records (see `decisions/`)
- Discovery reports and raw inventories (see `discovery/`)
- Unreviewed personal architecture sketches outside EAO process
