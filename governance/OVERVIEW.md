# Governance — Overview

## Purpose

House governance artefacts that define how architecture, standards, reviews, and enterprise transformation work are controlled across the EduVijna ecosystem.

## Scope

- Policies, process definitions, and compliance-oriented guidance for EAO activities
- Contribution, review, and escalation process artefacts
- Cross-cutting controls that apply to work stored in this repository

## Ownership

EduVijna Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO), with stakeholder input from engineering and product leadership as required by EAO process.

## Contents

Governance policies, process definitions, and related control artefacts.

## Exclusions

- Application-level runtime policies and infrastructure configuration
- Legal contracts and HR policies unrelated to architecture governance
- Architecture designs, standards catalogues, ADRs, and discovery reports themselves
